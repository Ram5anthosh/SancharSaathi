KYI allows users to check the details of wireline Internet Service Providers (ISPs) available in their area. This helps users choose the best ISP for their needs.

Steps to use KYI:

  • Visit the Sanchar Saathi portal and select "KYI."
  • Enter your location details.
  • View the list of ISPs in your area.
