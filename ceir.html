The Central Equipment Identity Register (CEIR) helps users block and trace lost or stolen mobile devices. By registering a lost phone, the IMEI number is blocked across all telecom networks, rendering the device unusable. If someone tries to use the blocked phone, its location becomes traceable. The device can also be unblocked when recovered.

Steps to use CEIR:

  • Visit the Sanchar Saathi portal and select "CEIR."
  • Fill in your device details (IMEI, brand, model, etc.).
  • Upload a copy of the police complaint.
  • Block the IMEI of the lost phone.
  • Unblock the phone if recovered.
