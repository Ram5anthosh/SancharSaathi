Chakshu enables users to report suspected fraudulent communications, such as phishing attempts, financial scams, or fake KYC updates, through calls, SMS, or WhatsApp. Reporting helps the government take action against fraudsters.

Steps to use Chakshu:

  • Visit the Sanchar Saathi portal and select "Chakshu."
  • Select the type of fraud communication.
  • Fill in the required details and upload evidence (optional).
  • Submit your report.
