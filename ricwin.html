The RICWIN service helps users report international calls that appear to originate from Indian numbers. These calls often use illegal telecom setups and may pose security risks.

Steps to use RICWIN:

  • Visit the Sanchar Saathi portal and select "RICWIN."
  • Enter the details of the international call received.
  • Submit your report for government action.
